          How to play

          1. Study itThe object works once and holds still. Every 15 seconds it flashes up whole again, and Hint shows it once an object, for 1.5 seconds.
          2. Rebuild itIts parts land in the tray. Drag each one back where it goes.
          3. Turn itOne part arrives turned, two from Level 4. Double-tap it, or press R over it, to turn it back.
          4. ConfirmRight parts lock in. Wrong ones fly back and cost 3 seconds.
          5. Spot the decoyOne part comes from another object, and from Level 2 it takes on this one's colour. Leave it out.
          6. Make it workSome objects only count once you work them: turn the key, crank the handle.
          7. Climb the levelsAlone, rebuild 3 objects of a level to open the next. Six levels, more to put back each time.
